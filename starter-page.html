Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central fundamentals

Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central is a business management solution that automates and streamlines business processes and helps you manage your business. Highly adaptable and rich with features, including finance, manufacturing, sales, shipping, project management, services, and more.

User profile

Newcomers or users, who implement and configure Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central, including the settings for core functionality and modules, as well as data migration to Business Central.

Time

1 business day, approxiately 8 hours

Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central Finance

Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central Finance covers the configuration of financial management in Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central. You learn how to set up the general ledger, chart of accounts, posting groups, journal templates, currencies, dimensions, financial reports, and fixed assets.
