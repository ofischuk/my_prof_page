Microsoft Dynamics365 Business Central Sales

Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central Sales covers day-to-day sales and purchasing operations in Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central. You learn how to navigate the interface, create and process purchase orders and sales orders, handle prepayments, and manage inventory adjustments and transfers.

Familiarity with sales and purchasing

You learn how to set up trade parameters, manage customers, vendors, and items, configure warehouse management and inventory locations, and define pricing and discount structures.

    • Purchasing Price and Discount Configuration

  • Module 5: Operations

    • Selling items
    • Order commitments
    • Processing Financial Transactions
    • Inventory Costs
